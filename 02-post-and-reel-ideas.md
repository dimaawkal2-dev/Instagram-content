# Post and Reel Ideas (DRAFT)

Format notes: Reels are 9:16 with on-screen text, since most people watch without sound. Carousels are 1080×1350 (4:5). Put the hook in the first 1–2 seconds of a Reel and on slide 1 of a carousel.

---

## Week 1 (full outlines)

### Mon Reel: "They're not giving you a hard time"
- **Hook (on screen, 0–2s):** "Your toddler isn't giving you a hard time. They're having one."
- **Beat 2 (2–10s):** Talking to camera: "The part of the brain that handles calm and self-control isn't fully developed until our mid-20s."
- **Beat 3 (10–20s):** "So in a meltdown, your child literally can't 'just calm down.' They need to borrow *your* calm."
- **Beat 4 (20–28s):** "Next time: get low, lower your voice, and say less. Connection first, lessons later."
- **Closing text:** "Follow for more 'why kids do that' 🧠"
- **Visual idea:** You on the couch or kitchen table, natural light. Optional B-roll of toys or a messy floor.

### Wed Carousel: "5 things to say during a meltdown"
1. **Cover:** 5 things to say during a meltdown (and 3 to skip)
2. "I'm right here." *(Safety first)*
3. "You're really upset. That's okay." *(Naming the feeling helps)*
4. "Let's breathe together." *(Co-regulation)*
5. "You can be mad. I won't let you hit." *(Feelings okay, behavior has limits)*
6. "Do you want a hug or some space?" *(Gives a sense of control)*
7. **Skip these:** "Stop crying." / "You're fine." / "Big kids don't do that."
8. **CTA slide:** Save this for the next hard moment 💛 Which one will you try?

### Fri Reel: "The day I lost my cool"
- **Hook:** "I talk about calm parenting for a living. Here's what I do when I'm not calm."
- **Body:** Everyone yells sometimes. What matters most is the *repair*. Script: "I yelled earlier. That wasn't okay. I was frustrated, and it's not your fault. I love you."
- **Point:** Repair teaches kids that relationships survive conflict, and that's a powerful lesson.
- **Closing text:** "Repair > perfect. Tell me in the comments if you needed to hear this."
- *(If you don't have kids or prefer not to share personal stories, frame it as "what I tell every parent I work with.")*

## Idea bank (for future weeks)

**Reels**
- "3 phrases I'd ban from every playground" (and what to say instead)
- "Things psych school taught me about kids that surprised me" (good for the journey pillar)
- POV: "Your child says 'I hate you.' Here's what's really going on."
- "Screen time meltdowns: why turning off the iPad causes chaos"
- Stitch or duet a trending parenting clip: "What the psychology actually says"
- "Day in the life of a parenting consultant / psych student"
- Quick myth-buster series: "Myth or fact?" (time-outs, sugar and hyperactivity, praise)

**Carousels**
- "Praise that builds confidence vs. praise that doesn't"
- "Your child's age-by-age emotional milestones" (keep it general and cite sources)
- "How to talk to kids about big changes (new baby, moving, divorce)"
- "Calm-down corner: what to put in it"
- "5 signs your parenting is working (even if it doesn't feel like it)"
- Student win or testimonial (with written permission)

**Engagement posts**
- "This or that: bedtime battles or morning chaos?"
- "Finish the sentence: The hardest part of parenting right now is ___"
- Q&A Reel answering a real (anonymized) follower question

**Course and sales**
- "Who [COURSE NAME] is for (and who it isn't for)"
- Behind the scenes of building the course
- FAQ carousel: time commitment, ages covered, price, access
- Free mini-lesson Reel that ends with "the full lesson is in [COURSE NAME]"
