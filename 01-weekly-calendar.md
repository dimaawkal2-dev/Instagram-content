# Weekly Content Calendar (DRAFT)

## Content pillars

Rotate these so the feed mixes reach, trust and sales instead of selling every post.

| Pillar | What it is | Main goal | Share of posts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Why kids do that** | The psychology behind common behavior (tantrums, whining, "no!") | Followers + shares | ~35% |
| **2. Say this, not that** | Ready-to-use scripts and small tools | Saves + engagement | ~35% |
| **3. You matter too** | Parent guilt, burnout, staying calm, your own triggers | Comments + connection | ~15% |
| **4. Course and journey** | Your course, student wins, your psych training story | Sales + trust | ~15% |

## The weekly template (3 posts)

| Day | Format | Pillar | Why this slot |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Reel (20–40s) | 1. Why kids do that | Starts the week with your best reach format |
| **Wednesday** | Carousel (6–8 slides) | 2. Say this, not that | Carousels get saved, and saves keep a post showing up |
| **Friday** | Reel or single post | Weeks 1–3: pillar 3. Week 4: pillar 4 (course) | Relatable end-of-week post; one soft sell a month |

**Suggested posting time:** 8:00–9:00 pm (after bedtime) or 6:30–7:30 am. After 3–4 weeks, check Insights → *Most active times* and adjust.

**Stories (optional, 5 minutes a day):** reshare the day's post, add one poll or question sticker ("Bedtime or mornings: which is harder?"), and reply to answers by DM yourself. Stories don't count toward the 3-post target.

**Engagement (15 minutes, the day you post):** reply to every comment in the first hour, and leave 5–10 real comments on posts from other parenting accounts.
