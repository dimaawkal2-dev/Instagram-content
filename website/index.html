The program

8 weeks, one step at a time

One focused module a week, with short lessons you can fit around real family life.

  1. Week1

    Why kids do that

    Behavior as communication, the developing brain, and what your child actually needs.

  2. Week2

    Your calm comes first

    Spot your triggers, regulate yourself, and become the steady place your child leans on.

  3. Week3

    Big feelings & tantrums

    What to say (and skip) during meltdowns, and how to help kids name and handle emotions.

  4. Week4

    Boundaries without blame

    Warm and firm limits, giving choices, and following through without yelling.

  5. Week5

    Bedtime, mornings & routines

    Turn daily battle zones into predictable, calmer rhythms.

  6. Week6

    Siblings & everyday battles

    Stop playing referee and coach kids to solve problems together.

  7. Week7

    Screens, school & growing kids

    Screen-time transitions, homework, friendships, and staying connected as kids get older.

  8. Week8

    Repair & reconnection

    Repair after hard moments, build lasting connection, and make your own family plan.
