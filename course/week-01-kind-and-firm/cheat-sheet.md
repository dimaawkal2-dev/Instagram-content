# Kind AND Firm: Fridge Cheat Sheet
### No Perfect Parents · Week 1 (DRAFT)

**Kind** = respect for my child  **Firm** = respect for me and the situation
**Both at once.** Not one, then the other.

---

### 🔁 Swap "but" for "and"
~~"I know you're sad, but…"~~ → **"I know you're sad, and…"**

### 💬 Phrases to try

**Little kids (1–5)**
- "You want another cookie. And cookies are done for today."
- "You're so mad! And I won't let you hit. You can stomp instead."
- "It's hard to leave. And it's time to go. Hop or bear-walk?"

**Bigger kids (6–12)**
- "I love you, and the answer is no."
- "You want more time. And screens are off at 7. What's your plan?"
- "Homework is frustrating. And I trust you. Easy part first or hard part first?"

### 🙋 Decide what I will do
- "I'll read stories to kids in pajamas."
- "I'll drive when seatbelts are on."
- "I'll listen when you use a calm voice."

**Then follow through: kindly, calmly, no lecture.**

### 🧭 The 5-question compass
1. Does it help them feel they **belong**?
2. Is it **kind AND firm**?
3. Does it work **long-term**?
4. Does it teach a **life skill**?
5. Does it help them feel **capable**?

### ❌ What we're leaving behind
Threats · Shaming · Lectures · Giving in · "Because I said so"

---
💛 **This week's challenge:** one kind-and-firm sentence a day.
*Messed up? That's part of it. There are no perfect parents.*
